Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

How do I start a corporate meal program?

Submit a quote through the site or call our team. After a quick review of your team size, location and requirements, approved clients receive an ordering link and a tailored plan.

Which areas around Pune do you serve?

We operate across Balewadi, Baner, Hinjewadi, Wakad and the surrounding IT corridor, run from our central kitchen in Balewadi.

Do you cater to Jain, vegan or allergen-specific diets?

Yes. Our menus are pure vegetarian, with Jain, vegan and allergen-aware options available on request for both daily meals and events.

How does billing and GST work?

Every invoice is GST-compliant. Corporate meal programs are billed per plate with employer-subsidy handling; event catering is quoted upfront and transparently.

Can you handle large events and weddings?

Absolutely — from 50 to 5,000 guests, with multi-cuisine live counters, plated service and buffet setups across Pune.

How far in advance should I book an event?

For large events we recommend reaching out at least a week ahead so we can plan menus, tastings and staffing. Smaller orders can often be arranged on shorter notice.
